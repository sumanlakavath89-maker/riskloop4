Avoid Double Exposure

Buying EUR/USD & GBP/USD (+0.86) simultaneously doubles your short USD exposure. If the Dollar rallies, both trades take heavy drawdowns.

Effective Hedging

EUR/USD and USD/CHF have a near-perfect inverse correlation (-0.94). Holding long positions in both hedges directional Dollar volatility.

True Diversification

Pairs with low correlation like USD/JPY & AUD/USD (-0.22) move on independent drivers (BoJ yields vs commodities), diversifying portfolio risk.
